About Us

Our History

Pawsitive Pet Academy was established in 2023 in Durban by Sarah Smith. We have trained hundreds of pet owners and aspiring pet care professionals through our six-month programmes and six-week courses.

Our Vision

To be South Africa's most trusted pet care educational academy, where every pet owner gains knowledge to give their pets a healthy and happy life.

Our Mission

To provide practical, professional pet care training that improves the wellbeing of animals and creates job opportunities in the pet care industry.

Our Goals

  • Make quality pet education accessible to everyone
  • Reduce pet negligence through education
  • Grow a community of responsible pet owners
  • Help students build pet care careers
